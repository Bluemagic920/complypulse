Zero-PHI Architecture: Built for Healthcare Privacy

Audit staff access and unapproved AI tools without ever exposing patient records, emails, or clinical charts.

1. Zero Patient Data (ePHI) Touched

ComplyPulse never reads patient charts, EHR systems, email contents, or clinical files. It only checks staff login status, MFA protection, and third-party app permissions.

2. Read-Only Practice Access

Your IT support or MSP grants strictly read-only access (User.Read.All, AuditLog.Read.All, Application.Read.All). Credentials are held in memory and never stored.

3. Safe Offboarding & Record Retention

ComplyPulse flags departed clinicians or front-desk staff who still have active accounts—and reminds IT to preserve email retention holds before removing licenses.

4. Annual HIPAA Risk Analysis Proof

Download a timestamped PDF packet documenting your technical safeguards for OCR audits, cyber-insurance renewals, and annual HIPAA compliance reviews. Support: support@profitharborai.com.
